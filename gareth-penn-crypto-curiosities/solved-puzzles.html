2. 3 September 2020, Plymouth, New York (27 Aug – 2 Sep 2020)

Answer: the place is stated in plain text and the date is hidden in a number series: 9320 = 9/3/20, the day after the last tweet.

The "…" asks for the next term, base 10: 9320, read as a date 9/3/20 (3 September 2020). This is how Penn writes dates as numbers elsewhere: TIMES 17 → 9745 → 9-7-45 in Times 17; a Copyright Office registration titled "8790".

2b. "All the factors add up": why Plymouth

With Penn's letter values (A=1 … Z=26), PLYMOUTH = 16+12+25+13+15+21+20+8 = 130. Throughout his books, 130 is his number for the suspect's mother ("BER", for Berta). That makes the place itself part of his number vocabulary. This reading of "add up" is ours; the date and place are not in doubt.
