## Methods

### M: Binary passages of the 340 read as numbers / Morse (continuation from Part 1)
- Category: Morse/binary
- Rules: Penn has a fixed 0/1 "digital transcription" of the 340 (made by Jan 1981; the rule is given in Part 1). Character #n = digit #n. He picks out runs of digits and reads each run as (a) a binary integer, (b) a binary fraction, or (c) Morse (dit 0, dah 1) after "redividing" the run into letters.
- Examples:
  - Opening passage ERAHO (Morse 0 010 01 0000 111) = "fraction of pi" (pi in binary = 11.0010010000111...). It is followed by family two-letter codes UEIKMB, said to approximate the fraction of pi. MB is BM reversed. ANI (of "animal") after DANGERTUE is a redivision of PI = O'HARE. T17 p.74 (PDF p.1).
  - "Third digital passage" 0100100010100001111: first 7 digits = Morse RED (010 0 100) = "zero/circle". The rest, 0.010100001111, = 1/sqrt(10) in base 2. T17 p.74 (PDF p.1).
  - Crossed Circle #1 starts 10000010 = binary 130 (BER), immediately followed by 111010 = 58 (O'HARE). Crossed Circle #2 starts 100011 = 35 (BM). T17 p.75 (PDF p.2).
  - Line 7 passage printed as "11001010100.111", said to be "identical in form" to MIKADO = 11 00 101 01 100 111. [Printed string does not literally equal 110010101100111. Part 1 p.39 prints the passage as "110010101 100111". Likely a typo on p.77.] T17 p.77 (PDF p.4).
  - Chars #144-165 = 1100100110111001001101 (22 digits). The second 11 digits repeat the first 11 (P = 1/2^11 = 1/2048; "45,056" coin-flip trials vs. 165). Split as 1100 (12) + 1001101 (77). First digit is Character #144 = 12^2; last digit of the 11-digit unit is #154 = 77x2. "12 before 77" = 77-12 = 65; given twice -> 65x2 = 130 (BER). T17 p.78 (PDF p.5).
  - Six-digit passage at #299 = 100011 (35, BM); eight-digit passage at #307 = 10000010 (130, BER). This is the mirror order of the opening pair. T17 p.78-79 (PDF pp.5-6).
- What he claims it proves: the 340 is a "tapestry of digital expressions", non-linguistic, signed with O'Hare family codes. Filler is "thematically appropriate", like a crossword constructor filling a grid.
- Citation(s): T17 pp.74-79 (Part 2, PDF pp.1-6).

### M: Square root / reciprocal-of-square-root in base 2 ("mathematical curiosity")
- Category: numerology/arithmetic + Morse/binary
- Rules: In base b, sqrt(b) and 1/sqrt(b) have the same digits (only the point moves). That fails across bases. Finding 1/sqrt(10) written in base 2 "implies" you should write sqrt(10) in base 2 and redivide it as Morse.
- Examples:
  - sqrt(10) base 10 = 3.1622776, reciprocal 0.31622776. sqrt(2) base 2 = 1.0110101000001, reciprocal 0.1011010100001.
  - sqrt(10) in base 2 = 11.001010011000..., redivided 11 00 101 0 0110 00 = Morse MIKE PI.
- What he claims it proves: MIKE PI = Mike O'Hare (PI = short form of O'HARE).
- Citation(s): T17 p.74 (Part 2, PDF p.1).

### M: Misspelling -> Morse -> redivision
- Category: Morse/binary; word-play
- Rules: Take a misspelled or "inappropriate" word (errors mark significance), write it in Morse as one bit string with no letter gaps, and re-segment it into other Morse letters or binary numbers.
- Examples:
  - MINUTS = 11 00 10 001 1 000 -> 110 0 10 0 0110 00 = GENE PI. T17 p.74 (PDF p.1).
  - NEAD (Pen Card) = 10 0 01 100 -> 1000 11 00 = "BM (am) I" (= binary 140). T17 p.98 (PDF p.25).
  - NEW (x3: "new", "news", "news") = 10 0 011 = 100011 = 35 = BM. T17 p.98 (PDF p.25).
  - BUTON (Dragon Card, misspelled twice) = 1000 001 1 111 10 = binary 4222 = 42deg22' N, latitude of Cambridge MA. T17 p.106 & caption p.[102] (PDF pp.33, 29).
  - SWAMPEDOUT = 0 00 0110 11101 100 10 0 111001 1, labelled "x2 I P 29 D N E 57 -". Read in reverse unit order: 57, END, 29, PI, x2 (2 pi radians per circle). The trailing T (1) is filler "to make it look like English". T17 p.132 (PDF p.59).
  - "Around in the snow" (Lake Tahoe Card) is read as an instruction: write SNOW in Morse (000 10 111 011) and turn it around -> 110 111 01 000 -> 110 (ME) + 11101000 (binary 232 = 4 x 58 = Berta). T17 p.134 (PDF p.61).
  - BUSS (Mount Diablo Letter) = BUS + S. BUS is a digital synonym of THREE (both 1000001000). S = 000 appended = multiply by 8, so BUSS = "THREE x 8", echoed by ".38" on the next line. T17 pp.149-151 (PDF pp.76, 78).
- What he claims it proves: O'Hare family names and the radian are hidden in "errors".
- Citation(s): as listed.

### M: Morse sentence with S = "is" and crossed circle = 100 (MELVIN EATS BLUBER)
- Category: Morse/binary
- Rules: Write the whole phrase in Morse without spaces and regroup it into binary numbers and letters. S (000) acts as the verb "is" ('s). The crossed-circle sign is read as digits 100.
- Examples: MELVIN EATS BLUBER = 1100100 000100 100011 000100 0010 0001 10000010 = hundred, S+crossed circle(100), 35, S+crossed circle(100), F, V, 130 => "GENE'S 100, BM'S 100, FV, BER". Penn writes "hundred" because "100" is also binary 4. The two 000100 groups are a "parallelism", split 000 (S) + 100 (crossed circle). Penn calls it the only Zodiac use of "to be".
- What he claims it proves: same vocabulary as the angles at the foot of the Exorcist Letter (GENE 100, BM 35, FV, BER 130); both parents = 100 = crossed circle.
- Citation(s): T17 p.94 (Part 2, PDF p.21); caption T17 p.[102] (PDF p.29).

### M: Rebus subtraction "X before Y" = Y - X (also applied to bit strings)
- Category: numerology/arithmetic; Morse/binary
- Rules: A number placed before a word or number means subtract it from what follows. With a Morse word, remove the trailing bits that spell X in binary (equivalently (Y - X)/2^k).
- Examples:
  - MIKADO = 11 00 101 01 100 111. The last 7 bits 1100111 = 103. In the 340, MIKADO starts at Character #104, i.e. 103 digits precede it. So "103 before MIKADO" = MIKADO minus 103 = 11 00 101 0 = MIKE. Caption: "MIKE + 103 = MIKADO". P = 1/2^15 = 1/32,768. T17 p.77-78 (PDF pp.4-5).
  - Exorcist Letter: the I-dot of "Signed" to the upper dot of the colon after "truley" = 103 mm, followed by the Mikado quotation. Again "103 before MIKADO" = MIKE, placed where a signature belongs. T17 p.[90] and figure p.[91] (PDF pp.17-18).
  - "12 before 77" = 65 (340 chars #144-154). T17 p.78 (PDF p.5).
  - "Me 37": ME = Morse 110 = 6. "6 before 37" = 31 (O'Hare's age at his 31st birthday, the week before the letter; both parents' ages at marriage). T17 p.97, caption p.[92] (PDF pp.24, 19).
- What he claims it proves: the signature MIKE hidden by subtraction.
- Citation(s): as listed.

### M: Rebus division "X over Y" (physical stacking of measurements)
- Category: grid/geometry; numerology
- Rules: A measured length printed above another means numerator over denominator.
- Examples: Exorcist Letter I-dots: think -> Exorcist = 76 mm (upper line); Exorcist -> saterical = 38 mm (below); saterical -> comidy = 95 mm. "76 over 38 = 76 / 38 = TWO" and 95 = 1011111 = Morse T W O (1 011 111). So "76 over 38 (is) TWO" is right numerically and orthographically. Penn calls it a "tutorial", like the Arecibo message. P.S.: I-dots of "if"/"this" (line 1) = 95 mm; "will"/"something" (line 3) = 58 mm. "TWO over 58" = 2/58 = 1/29, the postmark date (29 January 1974).
- What he claims it proves: the letter teaches its own reading rules; 1/29 = its postmark.
- Citation(s): T17 p.[90], p.94; annotated figures p.[91]-[92] (Part 2, PDF pp.17-21).

### M: Invisible geometry -- mm distances between marks
- Category: grid/geometry; typography/handwriting
- Rules: Choose a class of marks tied to the errors: I-dots of misspelled words, all I-dots in a passage, periods, T-crosses, colons, the circle/crossed circle. Measure centre-to-centre in millimetres "in syntactic order" and read each length as a number (often via Morse/binary).
- Examples:
  - Exorcist Letter: 76, 38, 95, 103, 95, 58 (see entries above and below).
  - "tit willo" written three times: I-dots exactly 17 mm apart each time = "THREE TIMES 17". T17 p.94 (PDF p.21).
  - Bus Bomb Diagram: see the separate entry (95, 95, 144, 74-75, 45, 36, 90, 72 mm).
- What he claims it proves: O'Hare vocabulary encoded as dimensions.
- Citation(s): T17 pp.[90]-108 "Invisible Geometry", pp.[144]-151 (PDF pp.17-35, 71-78). Name: "I have called this technique 'invisible geometry' because the lines ... are only implied ... connect-the-dots" (T17 pp.106-108).

### M: Invisible geometry -- angles formed by joining marks or figure centres
- Category: grid/geometry
- Rules: Join chosen points (I-dots, periods, T-crosses, centre of the crossed circle, centres of drawn figures found by drawing diagonals) with straight lines, and measure the angles with a protractor. Read the angle as a number (code word, Morse-binary, date, letter number). Like figures are joined to like ("circles to circles, switches to switches").
- Examples (exact values):
  - Exorcist Letter figures (foot of page): 130deg (BER), 100deg (GENE), 35deg (BM). Strokes forming "FV" (V inverted) = WIZARD of UE. A small angle inside the 130deg = 122-123deg = supplement of the radian (see radian entry). A line through the two large dots meets the extended upper leg of the 130deg tangent to the W of the first "titwillo": dot gap 23 mm, angle 23deg, W = letter 23 => "Pole".   - Stine letter (14 Oct 1969), 7 periods joined in order (speaking. shirt. area. noise. cover. morning. out.): angles 40, 152, 74, 52, 44deg. Largest 152 = 10011000 = 100110 + 00 = "38, I" = "I (am) 38". T17 p.98 (PDF p.25); figure p.[99] (PDF p.26).

  - Dragon Card (28 Apr 1970): angle BUTON - crossed circle - BUTON = 76deg = 38 x 2. T17 p.106, caption p.[102] (PDF pp.33, 29).
  - Bus Bomb Letter p.1 (20 Apr 1970): I-dots of "meannie", "cid", "teritory" form a triangle with angles 112deg (Morse OH) and 35deg (BM). Caption "THREE LITTLE WORDS". T17 p.106, figure p.[103] (PDF pp.33, 30).

### M: Differences between consecutive angles
- Category: numerology/arithmetic; Morse/binary
- Rules: Take the chain of angles (from joining periods) and subtract neighbours, always relative to the dominant angle when adjacent. Read the differences in binary/Morse; fuse adjacent small results.
- Examples (Stine letter): 152-40 = 112 = 1110000 = Morse OH. 152-74 = 78 = 1001110 = Morse N A M E (10 01 11 0). 74-52 = 22 = 10110 and 52-44 = 8 = 1000; fused 101101000 = 360 = "circle".
- What he claims it proves: "OH NAME (circle)", i.e. O'Hare's name.
- Citation(s): T17 pp.98 (Part 2, PDF p.25).

### M: Collinearity / extension of strokes as pointers
- Category: grid/geometry
- Rules: Extend a drawn stroke (a crossed-circle arm, a long stroke inside a figure) or a line through two punctuation points. If it hits another significant point exactly, treat the hit as intentional and use it as a vertex.
- Examples:
  - Bus Bomb Diagram: the vertical stroke of the crossed circle extends through the long stroke of the figure in the "Timer". The horizontal stroke extends through the centre of the zero in "SFPD = 0". T17 p.[144] (PDF p.71).
  - Mount Diablo Letter: the period after "buss" to the period after "38" extends through the I-dot of "untill". The period after "buss" to the decimal point before "38" extends through the second triangle (Character #12 of the 32-cipher, printed at the foot). The resulting triangle has 38deg at the cipher triangle and 122deg at the "untill" I-dot. Penn says it is "exactly similar" to the Boston Joan Webster triangle (38deg at the bus terminal BM 9, 122deg at the Logan Eastern terminal). 122 is linked to O'Hare's 38th birthday on 1-22-1981. T17 p.151 (PDF p.78); figure p.[150] (PDF p.77).
- What he claims it proves: a prefiguration (in June 1970) of the 1981 Webster crime.
- Citation(s): as listed.

- Category: grid/geometry; numerology
- Rules: Find the "key". In "crossed circle = 10" the equals sign points at where the circle's tag-ends cross, 10 mm from the circle centre, so all dimensions are in mm. Join the five circles (two suns, Timer, crossed circle, the 0 of SFPD=0) centre to centre. Penn says the H of "sunlight" (up-tick) looks like an Arabic 2 and the u of "sun" like a mirrored 2 (mirror bookends), so he joins those 2's to the Timer centre and bisects the lines inside the suns. Then he joins the four "swiches" centre to centre, and pairs bus-bus and car-car battery to the centre of "String of Bombs" (diagonals cross on the crossbar of the F in "of").
- Examples:

### M: 32-Character Cipher four-line Morse reading (Roman letters only)
- Category: cipher (classical) / Morse/binary
- Rules: Ignore non-alphabetic symbols, put the 23 Roman letters into Morse (reversed K = K, reversed F = F, the hooked symbol after V = J), and split into four "lines" marked by symbols (#19 dotted circle, #29 crossed circle). Redivide each line.
- Examples (Roman letters in order: CJIOKAMFORTGXFDVJHCELPW):
  - INTEGER-LINE: C J I O K A M F O R T G X = 1010 0111 00 111 101 01 11 0010 111 010 1 110 1001 (38 digits), redivided 10100 111001 111010 1110010 1110101 1101001 = 20, 57, 58, 114, 117, 105. 57/58 bound the radian (57.29...). 114 = 57x2 (= 57 + zero suffix) and also 38x3 ("Boston episode"). 117 = 58x2 + 1 (Riverside). The framing groups 10100...1101001 give 101001 101001 = 10 1001 10 1001 = binary 2, 9, 2, 9 (radian .29), split into groups of 5 and 7 digits (5, 7 -> 57).
  - PARENTS-LINE: F D V J H = 0010 100 0001 0111 0000 (19 digits = 38/2; running total 57) -> 001 0 1000 0 010 111 0000 = U E B E R O H = UE (Eugene), BER (Berta), OH (O'Hare).
  - FRACTION-LINE: C E L = 1010 0 0100 -> 10 10 00 10 0 = "2 NINE" = 29, just before the crossed circle (#29). "29" is thus given "two different ways".
  - SIGNATURE: P W = 16, 23 -> 1623 = 11001010111 -> 11 00 101 0 111 = MIKE O.
  - The first 18 letters (CJIOKAMFORTGXFDVJH) = 57 digits, then "29" = 57.29.
