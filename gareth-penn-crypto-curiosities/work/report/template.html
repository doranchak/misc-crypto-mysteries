Gareth Penn's crypto curiosities

A catalog of every method Gareth Penn used, from 1981 to 2022, to "read" hidden messages in the Zodiac letters and to tie them to his suspect, Michael O'Hare. The catalog has interactive re-implementations and citations. After it comes an attack on the puzzles Penn posted on his blogs and on Twitter/X from 2010 to 2025.

Compiled {{DATE}} from the collection "Zodiac/Solutions/Gareth Penn" (Times 17, The Second Power, Mensa Bulletin, The Cryptogram, blog posts, letters, FBI files, 2022 emails) plus web research. Penn's statements are reported as claims. Nothing here supports the accusation against O'Hare, who was never charged.

The 17-column grid: positions, coordinates, intersections, triangles, calendars

Penn (2010–2022) lays every Zodiac text out 17 characters wide, keeping only letters and digits. He then reads character numbers, (row, col) pairs as numbers, row × column intersections, rectangle areas, right-triangle hypotenuses √(Δr²+Δc²), and calendars (one character = one day from a dated "anchor").
