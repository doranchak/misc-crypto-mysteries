  • "Portrait of the Artist as a Mass Murderer", California (formerly New West), Nov 1981, pp.111–114, 166–170, as "George Oakes". The cover line was "A TALE OF OBSESSION", and the editors added a postscript ("There was too much flexibility in Oakes's technique"). P.166: "Galileo's discovery of the phases of Venus and Huygens's discovery of a moon on Saturn were originally published as anagrams." This is Penn's first published statement of the priority-anagram idea. A scan was posted to r/ZodiacKiller (2024; the post was removed, but the images survive) and is saved in work/extract/portrait1981/.
  • Pseudonyms: "George Oakes" and "Garfield Shaeffer" ("Catbird Seat", Ecphorizer Oct 1982; Sheaffer is a fountain-pen maker, so this is a pen-name pun on Penn). In Morse-binary: "Otherwise known as 110011001010" = G PENN (Ecphorizer Dec 1981 contributor note), and MIKE REVNEC = GARETH PENN (2010).
  • Penn the puzzle-setter. Editors' notes say he devised, for the Mensa Asilomar gathering, "a hellishly contrived puzzle involving a jigsaw puzzle, a Latin anagram, etc. It took 500 Mensans more than 30 hours to solve it". He also wrote the gathering program, in which "the next cryptogram tells you what time dinner will be served". The texts were not found.
  • Letter to the Webster family, 3 Mar 1982: the Zodiac's puzzle was "a kind of time-capsule. The puzzle was meant to be solved, but at some remove in time… maybe fifty years afterward" (r/UnsolvedMurders image; saved in work/extract/other_sources/).
  • "Penn's Kooky Kodes", a 14-figure set of 17-column grids sent to Tom Voigt in about 2007 (figs 1–7, 8–14).
  • Other leads not obtained: Nature letters "US health reform" (371:646, 1994) and "New world, old word" (376:546, 1995); Integra, "The world according to Dallas" (1988); the withdrawn 1999 Cryptologia paper; Penn's enclosures to Martin Gardner (1981, 1984; Stanford, Gardner papers); "Son of One Zero Zero" (announced 1984, apparently never printed); Boston Herald 29 Oct 1987 and Beverly Times 12 Jun 1990 articles.
