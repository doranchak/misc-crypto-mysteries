• Why Penn's method "always works". Every word has two or more numerical values (alphabetic, Morse-binary, Roman). Morse bit-strings can be re-cut ("redivided") freely, even through letters. Numbers can be read as dates in several orders, as day-of-year, as clock times, as angles mod 360°, or after adding, subtracting ("X before Y"), dividing ("X over Y"), squaring and doubling. Positions can be counted in characters, Morse digits, rows, columns or millimetres. Any long text therefore contains thousands of hits on a vocabulary of a few dozen "significant" numbers. The decoder and redivision finder show this. Penn's odds ("1 in 1.25 million") price one pre-chosen hit, but he searched for hits after the fact. The FBI Laboratory said the same in 1981–83. It recognized his "'Wizard' and 'Binary Morse' substitutions" as standard techniques, but found "the bulk of the theory… based on speculation and a multitude of assumptions" (26 Jun 1981). Penn "selectively dissects the literature (often misquoting material) and presents results only if they corroborate the overall theme of his solution… the results are forced and inconsistent" (lab report of 24 Jan 1983 on his 119-page 1982 packet; FBI Zodiac file extracts in the collection).
  • Penn contradicts himself across sources (found while merging the catalog). Each version is presented as the designed reading:
    • Riverside Timex: stopped at 12:22 (T17) vs 12:24 (SP; 1224 = 72 × 17).
    • Calibers: .22, 9 mm, .38 ("22 before 9" → .38, T17) vs only .22 and 9 mm (GUN = 22 × 9, 2010/2022).
    • The "radian" letter: postmarked 28 June 1970 (6.28 = 2π, 1985) vs 29 June (6/29 = 37 × 17, 2022).
    • Day-of-year 314: assigned to the 340 mailing (T17, 1985) vs the Death Machine letter (SP).
    • The 32-symbol cipher bits cut as 20, 57, 58, 114, 117, 1682, 130, 112 (Cryptogram 1986) vs 20 … 117, 105, then UE BER OH (T17).
    • "& #": 73 Morse digits (T17) vs 73 radians = 4183 = BERTA M (SP) vs 7³ (2022).
    • O'Hare's student magazine "Cambridge 38", Feb 1964 (T17) vs "Cambridge 28", June 1964 (1985).
    • Parents married 1938 (T17) vs 1937 implied by "Me 37" (1985).
    • The "DES JULY AUG SEPT OCT 7" box-score: summed to 7 (T17) vs read as character #219 = MAY (2022).
    • The "a Friend" signature: π to 11 bits (FRIEND, 1985) vs about 0.29 of a radian (AFRI, 1987).
    • Morse polarity: "and vice versa" (Laube interview, 1981) vs dah = 1 everywhere later.
  • Penn's arithmetic slips (flagged on the cards): ME 43 = 427, not 350; "BM 80" whose tail 101000 is 40; five call times summed to 3744 that actually sum to 3743; "exactly 15 years" = 5478 days, not 5479; 37/27 = 1.370, not 1.38; four inches = 101.6 mm (only his 101.595 gives MIK.TIMES2); TIMEX printed as 627 (Ecphorizer #53; it is 617); DONUT JAM is 20 binary places of φ, not 19.
  • Privacy and fairness. Penn's books include private data (phone numbers, addresses, a Social Security number) that he "decoded". These are deliberately omitted here. Crimes are referred to by place and date only. Michael O'Hare was never charged. Penn's predictions about him never came true.
  • Citations. "T17 p.N (Part k, PDF p.m)" means Times 17, printed page N, in file "times-17-part-k.pdf", page m. "SP" means The Second Power: printed pages = PDF−5 up to p.17 and PDF−4 from p.19, because printed p.18 is missing from the PDF and survives only as a separate Drive scan. Every example in the catalog was checked against the page images where it mattered, and the Morse/binary arithmetic was recomputed.
