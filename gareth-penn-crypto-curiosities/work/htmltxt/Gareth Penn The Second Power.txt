David, Di: 

Attached is Gareth Penn's second book, The Second Power:  A Supplement to Times 17.   It's so rare only two people I know have read it -- me and Jake Wark.  It was published in 1999, 12 years after Times 17.   You'll need to download JZip to open.  It's free and easy.   [LINK http://www.jzip.com/] http://www.jzip.com/

The files are jpg images of the pages -- easy to read and open.   Roughly 60 pages + appendices.   Thought this would take a while to put in a zip file, but it took a few seconds. 

I've found a good way to read Penn's "binary Morse" missives -- this book, Times 17, and some of his articles -- is to skim the stuff about, ciphers, and math, and focus on concrete material -- what I call "lucid sections."    You'll find lucid sections on pages 33-34, 40, 43-44, 46, 47, and 57 that get at the ghastly heart of this book.  

I found Second Power significant because it purports to be a detailed, highly-personal explanation of the killer's motives.  Wark found it -- as I think many readers would -- distasteful, even grotesque.   Learning what I did about Gareth's own childhood, it made sense to me that he was writing about himself through his usual surrogate, Mike O'Hare (Gareth did broach some of the childhood physical abuse he suffered at the hands of his stepfather in our interviews, however.)   

He didn't want the book widely distributed (apparently on second thought) and so it remains rarely seen to this day.   

Best,

Mike
