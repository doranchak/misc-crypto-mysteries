David:  

Here’s the promised timeline, a bit abbreviated.    Attached are divorce papers from Penn’s attorney referenced herein.   

March-April 1968 – Gareth and wife Mary Ann are in Germany, just as he is ending his Army commitment.   Mother suffers “psychotic break”.   Has to be institutionalized for a time. 

August, 1968-summer 1969:    Gareth and Mary Ann return from Europe.   They reside at 2804 Webster Street, Berkeley.   He studies at UC Berkeley.   She takes a job teaching at People’s Continuation High School at 233 Hobbs Ave in Vallejo.   Stays with Vallejo Public Schools for 20 years.    Witnesses have confirmed what Gareth also told me, that they attended Christmas and school parties at the Superintendent’s home in Benicia.   Gareth frequently drives to Vallejo to drop off and pick Mary Ann up when he needs their one car, a 1968 white VW beetle.

August 1969-73:    They move to Vallejo, starting an 11 year, 2 month residency in Vallejo and Napa (see timeline from divorce papers).   During this time, Mary Ann starts teaching at Solano Junior High, now Solano Middle, at 1025 Corcoran Ave, Vallejo, CA   In 1972, after getting his Berkeley MLS, Gareth starts his 7 year job at Vallejo Public Library, working in the Donovan McCune Room and doing reference work.  

Summer of 1969 through December 1972:   address unknown, Vallejo  
January to May 1973:   701 Capitol Street, Vallejo  

Old timers from Vallejo remember loving Mary Ann, who became a well-respected teacher, but finding Gareth abrasive and arrogant.   I spoke to three of their friends and two of his library co-workers from the period 1968-78.   His Vallejo Library supervisor Julie Stratton, and Pam Keith, a now-prominent Vallejo political figure, were the most outspoken about Gareth.   Amazing, since it was so many years ago!
