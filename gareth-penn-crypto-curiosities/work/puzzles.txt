(updated) (and tweets)
NOTE: On twitter, he tends to delete previous “puzzles” whenever posting a new one.




Gareth Penn was been [writing about the Zodiac
Killer](http://www.zodiackillerciphers.com/?p=543) for decades, claiming that Berkeley professor
Michael O’Hare is the notorious [cryptogram-authoring serial
killer](http://zodiackillerciphers.com/wiki/index.php?title=Main_Page). Very few people take his
claims seriously, especially because he abuses mathematics to generate coincidences that link
O'Hare to the killings.


Over the years, Penn has authored his own cryptograms and challenged his readers to solve
them. As far as I know, nobody has figured them out. Every so often, Penn posts his puzzles at
this site: [http://gz4216.blogspot.com/](http://gz4216.blogspot.com). Below are all of Penn's
puzzles I've collected. Can you crack them?
