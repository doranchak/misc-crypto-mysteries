2 · The key

Every letter has exactly one triple. The first two numbers pick one of three banks (4k–5k for k = 6, 7, 8); the third number rises by 6 for each next letter of the alphabet. Dashed cells never occur in the message: Q and X are interpolated from the pattern, Z is extrapolated.

4 · “Some math”: the key as one formula

The key is compact enough to write as a single affine map. This is my description of the table — it reproduces all 25 letters A–Y exactly — not something I could confirm the author used.

x = position in alphabet (A = 0 … Z = 25)
v = 3x + 14, then q = ⌊v / 29⌋, r = v mod 29
token = (24 + 4q) – (30 + 5q) – (19 + 2r)

Read it as: multiply the letter number by 3 and add 14, in arithmetic mod 29. Each time the running value wraps past a multiple of 29 you step to the next bank (24-30 → 28-35 → 32-40), and the third number is 19 + 2r (always odd; 19…75). The wraps fall right after E and O (and would after Y), which is why the banks hold 5, 10 and 10 letters.

Equivalent “no-modulo” form: third number = 6n + 41 (n = 1 for A), minus 58 each time it would exceed 76. Same table; the mod-29 form just says it in one line.

6 · What is and isn’t established

  • Solid: all 172 tokens (23 distinct) decode with no unknowns, to fluent English with no punctuation or numerals; the decode follows from the fixed arithmetic pattern, not from tuning to a desired plaintext.
  • Interpolated: Q (32-40-27) and X (32-40-69) don’t occur, but sit in gaps of otherwise unbroken runs of 6.
  • Extrapolated: Z. The three banks hold 5 + 10 + 10 = 25 letters; the formula puts Z in a fourth bank (36-45-23), but nothing in the message confirms that. The author may have handled Z differently, or not at all.
  • Not seen: I could not load the Reddit thread (it serves a login wall to my client), so I haven’t read the author’s own explanation of the “math” or any comments. The formula in §4 is a fit to the table, not a reconstruction of their method.
