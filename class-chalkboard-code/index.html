Weird code on class chalkboard: deciphered

Source: “Weird code on class chalkboard”, r/Decoders, posted by u/Heyxlistenx. Photo from that post.
Solved: a home-made “look-alike” alphabet, one glyph per letter

What it says

THE 10 COMMANDS! I SHALL NOT STILL [sic: the commandment is “steal”] I SHALL NOT KILL I SHALL NOT COMMIT ADULTERY WE US A STUDENT REMEMBER ♥

Every glyph on the board fits one key, with no leftovers. The two odd spots, STILL and “WE US”, are written that way on the board and are not decoding errors (see loose ends).

How it was cracked

Three lines start with the same run of glyphs, ! 8H577 nō⊥: a 1-letter word, a 5-letter word ending in a double letter, and a 3-letter word. That run is I SHALL NOT, and the words after it confirm it: ꓘ!77 = KILL and Ↄōmm!⊥ 5ᗡ∩7⊥≡12 4 = COMMIT ADULTERY. The heading ⊥H≡ 10 Ↄōmm5nᗡ8! then reads THE 10 COMMANDS!.

Those three lines give the key for 16 letters. The last two lines then read straight off it (WE US A STUDENT / REMEMBER). The only new glyphs there are W, which is drawn normally, and 13 = B, which is drawn like the 12 = R next to it.

There is no key, shift or transposition. Each letter is just drawn in a disguised form: turned upside down, mirrored, broken into pieces, or swapped for a digit that looks a bit like it.

Loose ends

  • STILL, not STEAL. The word is 8⊥!77 = S‑T‑I‑L‑L. The writer's E (≡) appears 7 times elsewhere and is not used here, so the misspelling is on the board, not in the decoding. It is either a slip (“steal”/“still”) or a joke.
  • “WE US A STUDENT”. ∩ must be U, because it is the U in both ADULTERY and STUDENT. So ∩8 can only be US, and the line really does say “WE US A STUDENT / REMEMBER”. The board does not show what was meant (maybe “we, as a student, remember…”, or deliberately broken English to match the STILL joke).
  • COMMANDS, not COMMANDMENTS. Only eight glyphs follow the 10: Ↄ ō m m 5 n ᗡ 8. A small ✓ touches the foot of the D. It belongs to the trail of check marks below, not to the word.
  • Check marks. About six ✓ marks trail down from the end of line 1, across the end of STILL, toward the trees. None of them is needed for any word. They may be someone ticking the STILL line, or birds belonging to the moon-and-pines drawing.
  • Specks under the lines are irregular chalk crumbs. They do not line up with the letters and carry no pattern. The dark band behind THE is an eraser smear. The chalk over it is crisp, so the smear came first. In THE the T's two strokes don't touch and the H's crossbar is only a dot; on the other lines H has a full crossbar.
  • Small glyph variants. The I in line 4 is a plain stroke with no dot. The first R of REMEMBER has its “2” redrawn. The Y at the end of ADULTERY runs into the base of the left pine.
