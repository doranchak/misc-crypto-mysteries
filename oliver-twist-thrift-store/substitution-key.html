Source of the key. Page 1 of the book comes with a partial key (blue letters over the glyphs); every other letter was checked glyph by glyph in words on pages 2–3 and on the cover pages. Two examples are shown per letter; the hand varies a little.
Cautions. J, V and X are rare; Q and Z have not been seen. N and E are sometimes cut in two by the joins. Touching neighbours (IN, IS, AN …) are not special glyphs. Doubles and digits: slide 2.

Where the digits come from. The back cover carries a row of ten block glyphs. The page-1 key reads the year as “1 9 9 ?”: the first glyph matches the first glyph of the row, and the two repeated glyphs match the ninth, so the row runs 1…9, 0. The fourth year glyph is then the sixth in the row, 6. This is confirmed by the two headings on spread 3, which read 960611.18:31 and 960611.18:11 (YYMMDD.HH:MM): year 96, month 06 = June (page 1 says “June ninth”), day 11, and times 18:11 and 18:31. The title page repeats “1996”. Digits 2, 3, 4, 5, 7 and 8 rest on the row order only (3 and 8 occur in the time stamps).
Doubled letters. EE, TT, LL, SS, FF, CC and OO are each drawn as one glyph that looks like the letter with an extra stroke (SEEM, KEEP, FEEL · LITTLE, SITTING · STILL, REALLY · MESS, PASSING · OFFSPRING · ACCIDENTAL · GOOD, TOO). PP in APPARENTLY is written as two separate P's, and a few other doubles are ambiguous, so this list is only the pairs seen at least three times with a consistent shape.
Not listed. CK looks fused in one BACK but separate in the other, so it is left out. ION and similar endings are two touching glyphs, not a special one.
